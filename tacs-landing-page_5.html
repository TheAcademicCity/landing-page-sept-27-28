Common Questions

Frequently asked by parents

Can the whole admissions process be completed online?
Yes, the application, entrance assessment, counselling and the conversation with our principal can all be completed fully online if a campus visit isn't possible right away.
Is TACS residential-only, or is day schooling available?
TACS is exclusively residential; we don't currently offer day-scholar or week-boarding options.
Can my child join TACS if they're currently on a different board?
Yes, switching boards is allowed at any grade level except Grades 10 and 12, where board continuity matters most.
When can we visit the campus?
Any day of the week, 9 AM to 6 PM. An appointment is preferred so our team can plan your visit, but it isn't required.
What's the policy on phones and gadgets?
Personal gadgets aren't permitted in the hostels; students get supervised internet access at specified times, and family calls are scheduled through the dorm parent.
What if my child has specific medical or learning needs?
We're happy to discuss individual requirements; please reach out to our admissions team directly so we can understand how best to support your child.
What happens in a medical emergency?
Our campus has full-time nursing staff and an on-call doctor round the clock, backed by partnerships with nearby hospitals for anything beyond routine care.
